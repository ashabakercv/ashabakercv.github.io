# Human review rubric

| Dimension | Accept | Revise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grounding | Each factual claim maps to the notes | Unsupported date, owner, approval or outcome |
| Decision fidelity | Pending decisions remain pending | A question or recommendation becomes a commitment |
| Completeness | Material blockers and next steps remain visible | A blocker or required action disappears |
| Usability | Recipient can identify what needs attention | Vague action or unclear audience |

Record the reviewer, date, source fixture and rationale. Use “not reviewed” until human review occurs. Keep a failed example with its correction so later changes can be checked against the original error.
